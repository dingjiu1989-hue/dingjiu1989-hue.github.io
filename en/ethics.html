Code of Ethics

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Editorial Independence

AI Study Room maintains full editorial independence. Our content decisions are made solely by our editorial team, free from external influence by advertisers, partners, or other interested parties.

Conflicts of Interest

We require all contributors to disclose any potential conflicts of interest. When reviewing or comparing products, tools, or services:

  • We disclose any financial or professional relationship with the product or company being reviewed.
  • We do not accept payment for positive reviews.
  • Affiliate commissions do not affect our rankings or recommendations.
  • Sponsored content is clearly labelled and kept separate from editorial content.

Objectivity and Fairness

  • Comparisons present balanced facts about all options, not just the recommended one.
  • We clearly distinguish between facts, opinions, and sponsored content.
  • Multiple perspectives are presented on controversial topics.
  • We correct errors promptly and transparently (see our Corrections Policy).

Privacy and Sources

  • We protect the confidentiality of sources who request anonymity.
  • We do not publish private information without consent.
  • User data collected through our site is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

Plagiarism and Originality

Plagiarism is strictly prohibited. All content is original or properly attributed. We use plagiarism detection tools to verify originality of published content.

Accountability

We are accountable to our readers. If you believe we have violated this code of ethics, please contact us at dingjiu1989@gmail.com. All reports are investigated promptly and thoroughly.

Compliance

All contributors and editors are required to adhere to this Code of Ethics. Violations may result in content removal, retraction, or removal of contributor privileges.
