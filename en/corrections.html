Corrections Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Our Commitment to Accuracy

AI Study Room is committed to publishing accurate, fact-checked content. Despite our best efforts, errors can occur. When they do, we correct them promptly and transparently.

How We Handle Errors

Minor Errors (typos, formatting, broken links)

Fixed silently and promptly. The article's metadata is updated with a new "last modified" date.

Significant Errors (factual mistakes, misattributions, outdated information)

  • Correction notice: A note is added at the top of the article explaining what was corrected and when.
  • Strikethrough for removed content: Where context matters, the original error is shown with strikethrough rather than silently deleted.
  • Changelog entry: The correction is logged in the article's metadata.

Retractions

If an article contains a fundamental error that invalidates its main thesis, the article may be retracted. A retraction notice is placed at the top, and the article remains accessible for transparency but is clearly marked as retracted.

Reporting an Error

If you find an error in any of our content, please contact us at dingjiu1989@gmail.com with:

  • The article URL
  • A description of the error
  • Your source or reference for the correction (if applicable)

We aim to respond within 48 hours and resolve verified errors within 5 business days.

AI-Generated Content Corrections

Some content on this site is AI-assisted (see our Editorial Standards for details). All AI-assisted content is reviewed by a human editor before publication. If an error originating from AI assistance is identified, it is corrected with the same rigor as any other error.

Corrections Log

We maintain a running log of all significant corrections made to our articles. For a complete history, contact us at dingjiu1989@gmail.com.
