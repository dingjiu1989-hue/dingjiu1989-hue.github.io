Editorial Standards & AI Disclosure

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Our Mission

AI Study Room publishes practical, actionable developer content — tutorials, comparisons, news digests, and guides — in both English and Chinese. Our goal is to help developers build better software and grow their careers with unbiased, well-researched information.

AI Usage Disclosure

We believe in transparency about our content creation process:

  • News digests (Daily): AI tools assist in summarising and translating news from multiple sources. A human editor reviews all content before publication.
  • Tutorials and guides: Written by human authors. AI tools may be used for research assistance, proofreading, or translation between English and Chinese. All technical content is verified by the author.
  • Comparisons and reviews: Hands-on testing by human reviewers. AI may assist in structuring the comparison but not in forming the opinion.
  • Image generation: Article cover images are AI-generated, clearly identifiable as illustrative art.

Key principle: All content published on AI Study Room is human-curated. AI is a tool, not a replacement for editorial judgment. Every article is reviewed, edited, and approved by a human before publication.

Content Standards

  • Originality: All content is original. We do not plagiarise or republish others' work without attribution.
  • Accuracy: Facts, code samples, and technical claims are verified before publication.
  • Attribution: Sources are cited for statistics, quotes, and third-party claims. Code snippets from other projects include licensing attribution.
  • Timeliness: Articles are reviewed periodically. Outdated content is updated, consolidated, or removed.
  • Independence: Opinions and recommendations are the author's own. Affiliate links are clearly disclosed. Sponsored content is marked as such.

Affiliate & Monetization Disclosure

AI Study Room may use affiliate links and display ads (Google AdSense). We only recommend products and services we genuinely believe provide value to developers. Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial content or rankings. Sponsored content will be clearly labelled as "Sponsored" or "Paid Content."

Translation Policy

Content is published in both English and Chinese. Translations are human-reviewed to ensure accuracy and cultural appropriateness. English and Chinese versions may have slight differences to better serve each audience, but the core information remains consistent.
